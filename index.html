Districts

Pick a district, then click or drag across tiles to paint them. Click a painted tile again to remove it.

How to play
  • Each hex is a neighborhood. The big number is its population; the percentage is the share of voters expected to vote for your candidate.
  • Split the whole map into the required number of districts. Every tile must belong to a district.
  • Each district must be one connected piece and its population must be within ±5% of the ideal size.
  • Your candidate wins a district when more than 50% of its voters support them.
  • Win at least the target number of districts, even though your candidate has fewer votes statewide.
  • Shortcuts: 1–8 pick a district, E eraser, V switch view.
