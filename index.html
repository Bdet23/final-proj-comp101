How to play
  • Each hex is a neighbourhood with a population and a share of people who vote for your candidate. Hover over a tile for details.
  • Split the whole map into the required number of districts. Every tile must belong to a district.
  • Each district must be one connected piece and its population must be within the allowed range.
  • Your candidate wins a district when more than 50% of its voters support them. The badge on each district shows its result: green means you lead.
  • Win at least the target number of districts, even though your candidate has fewer votes statewide.
  • Real states: pick a state under "Real states" in the Map menu and choose a party. Each hex is a county, or part of a large county, with its real 2019 population estimate and 2020 presidential vote.
  • Realistic mode: each election the state, its regions and each tile can swing a few points. Badges show each district's chance to win, and you need a 70% chance of winning the target number of seats.
  • Shortcuts: 1–8 pick a district, E eraser, V cycle colours, L cycle tile numbers.
